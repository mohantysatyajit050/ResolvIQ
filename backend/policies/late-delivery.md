# Late delivery policy

## When an order counts as late
An order is late when it has not been delivered by the expected delivery date shown on the order. An order delivered on or before the expected date is not late. Cancelled and returned orders are never treated as late.

## What the customer can ask for
If an order is 3 or more days past its expected delivery date and has still not been delivered, the customer can cancel it and receive a full refund. If the order is less than 3 days late, we ask the customer to wait while we keep tracking the order.

## Orders that were already delivered late
If an order was delivered 3 or more days after its expected date, a support agent reviews the case and decides what is offered. This is never decided automatically.

## How the refund is paid
Approved refunds follow the refund timing policy.
