# Damaged item policy

## Reporting window
A damaged item must be reported within 24 hours of delivery. The time is counted from the delivery time recorded on the order. Reports made later are reviewed by a support agent instead of being decided automatically.

## Evidence required
The customer must attach at least one clear photo of the damaged item. A photo of damaged outer packaging helps, but it does not replace a photo of the item itself.

## Resolution
When the report is inside the window and has photo evidence, the customer can choose a replacement or a full refund for the damaged item. Orders above the approval limit are always checked by a support agent first.
