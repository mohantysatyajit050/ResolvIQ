# Refund timing policy

## When a refund starts
After a refund is approved, we start the refund within 12 hours.

## When the money arrives
The refund goes back to the original payment method. After we start it, the bank or payment provider usually takes 5 to 7 business days to show the money in the customer's account. We do not control the bank's processing time.

## Customers asking about a refund
If a refund was started less than 5 business days ago, the customer is asked to wait for the bank. If it was started more than 7 business days ago and the money has not arrived, a support agent checks the refund.
