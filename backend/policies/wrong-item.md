# Wrong item policy

## Reporting window
A wrong item must be reported within 48 hours of delivery, counted from the delivery time recorded on the order. Later reports are reviewed by a support agent instead of being decided automatically.

## What counts as a wrong item
The customer received a different product, size, colour or model than the order lists.

## Evidence required
The customer must attach a photo of the item received and, where it is visible, a photo of its label or packaging.

## Resolution
When the report is inside the window and has photo evidence, the customer can choose a replacement with the correct item or a full refund. Orders above the approval limit are always checked by a support agent first.
